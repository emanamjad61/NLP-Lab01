Google announced on Tuesday that the Pixel 10 would ship to over 40 countries by October 2025. Speaking at the launch event in Mountain View, California, chief executive Sundar Pichai said the company plans to launch the phone at a starting price of $799, which is $100 less than the latest iPhone from Apple. Analysts at Counterpoint Research expect the device to capture 5 percent of the premium smartphone market in the United States within its first year. Apple shares fell 2% in New York after the announcement, while Samsung said it would reveal its own response in Seoul next month.
